How this works

Consensus requires at least two named analysts on the same selection, exact line and scheduled game. Repeat posts by an analyst count once; their most recently observed selection is used. Staff and model bylines are excluded from analyst agreement.

The Props tab includes individual expert selections without requiring agreement. Player-only selections are matched using current ESPN rosters and an explicit article week. The homepage shows upcoming games only, prioritizing agreement across multiple publishers, then net support and supporter count. Picks with more tracked opposition than support do not appear here. Schedule failures pause homepage recommendations.

Expert parlays retain the publisher’s original numbered legs and combined odds. We do not multiply same-game leg prices or invent popularity data. Recent includes started games; Upcoming requires a verified future kickoff for every leg.

Prices come from source articles, not a live sportsbook. Missing prices remain blank. ESPN supplies schedule data. Public source changes can interrupt updates.
