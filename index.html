About these picks

The homepage ranks matching selections by distinct article bylines, then picks from publisher articles labelled “best bets” or “best picks”, then recency. This is an editorial shortlist, not a win probability or verified performance ranking.

Article bylines can differ from individual roundtable contributors. Only explicitly stated selections are extracted. Full articles may contain more picks. No live odds, game status or result grading is supplied.

Odds convert only when the source publishes a price. Missing odds stay blank. Prediction market contract prices remain in cents.

What makes a top pick?

Matching selections from more distinct article bylines come first, followed by publisher “best bets” or “best picks”, then the newest selections.

The homepage shows up to six picks published in the past 48 hours. Use All picks for the wider board.

“Best” is this shortlist’s sorting method. It does not mean better odds of winning. Verify kickoff and the original expert’s reasoning before betting.
